The Generalization Gap:
AI Perception vs SQL Logic.

ERPs like SAP, Oracle, and ERPNext demand perfection and strict schema integrity, but the physical warehouse supply chain is messy. Traditional zonal OCR breaks when vendor layouts drift. ERPYAS bridges this chasm by completely decoupling cognitive AI extraction from deterministic transactional accounting.

The Cost Bottleneck

SMEs waste hours on manual keying. We reduce document input latency from 48 hours to 4.5 seconds with mathematical certainty.

The HITL Shield

AI models can hallucinate. ERPYAS mathematically enforces docstatus: 0 (Draft Mode). The AI does the typing; the human retains legal authority.

The 10/90 Compute Split

We never send unprocessed raw images across unreliable networks. The on-device engine compresses and cleans each document before the cloud layer performs semantic reasoning.
