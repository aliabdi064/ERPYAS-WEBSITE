Delete your ERPYAS account

You can delete your ERPYAS account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ERPYAS and tap Settings (gear, bottom right).
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm. It happens at once.

Without the app

Email us from the address you sign in to ERPYAS with, subject "Delete my account". We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Email info@erpyas.com

We only act on requests sent from the account's own sign-in email, so nobody else can delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your ERPYAS account: sign-in, name, plan, credits and usage.
  • Your customer record in our admin records.
  • On your phone (when deleted in the app): your ERPNext keys, invoices still waiting to upload, and the undo history.
  • A paid plan is cancelled at once and does not renew.

What is kept, and why

  • Invoices already in your ERPNext. They are in your own system and belong to you; ERPYAS never deletes them.
  • Payment records at Stripe, our payment provider, as tax law requires.
  • A one-way code made from your email (not the email itself), so a deleted account cannot start a second free trial.
  • Messages you sent us through Feedback & support, to answer them and for our support history.
  • Server logs (30 days) and app crash reports (90 days), deleted automatically after that.
